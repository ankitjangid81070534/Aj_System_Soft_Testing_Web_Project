## On-page rules

- One primary query per page; use three to eight closely related concepts only where they help the reader.
- Titles and headings must read naturally. No hidden text, duplicate location pages, mass-generated landing pages or `meta keywords`.
- Industry and technology terms may be used only where the service or a real case study supports the claim.
- Every indexable page needs a unique title, description, canonical URL, useful heading structure and contextual internal links.
- Search performance should be reviewed by page/query in Search Console; refine content from real impressions and conversions, not by repeating phrases.
